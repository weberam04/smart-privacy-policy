Privacy Policy

S.M.A.R.T. Flagler — Effective Date: May 23, 2026

Overview

S.M.A.R.T. Flagler (Saving Missing Animals Response Team) is a community animal rescue reporting app serving Flagler County, Florida. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Account Information
When you sign in using Google authentication, we collect your email address and Google account identifier. This information is stored securely in Firebase, our cloud backend provided by Google.

Location Data
The app requests access to your device's location to help you pin the location of an animal report on the map. Location data associated with your report is stored in Firebase. We do not track your location in the background or outside of active use of the app.

Photos
The app requests access to your camera and photo library so you can attach photos to animal reports. Photos you submit are stored in Firebase as part of your report. We do not access your photo library beyond what you explicitly select to upload.

Animal Reports
Information you enter when submitting a report, including animal description, location, and photos, is stored in Firebase and may be visible to other app users and rescue coordinators for the purpose of locating and helping animals.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely to operate the S.M.A.R.T. Flagler app and facilitate animal rescue coordination. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

This app uses Firebase (Google) for authentication and data storage. Your use of the app is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data Retention

Your account and report data are retained in Firebase for as long as your account is active or as needed to operate the app. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
